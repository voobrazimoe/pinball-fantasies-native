## Ownership and harmless extras

Gameplay registry authority is `timing.Labels` for F1, `programs.Labels` for F2,
and `Display.Content.Labels` for F3/F4. The shared presentation registry separately
owns attract/high-score/player-selection/game-over programs.

The checker also verifies source frontend entry closure: 11 destinations for
F1/F2 and 10 for F3/F4, all present in the shared renderer registry. F1/F2 intercept
match/game-over in gameplay; `AFTER_XXBALLTS` is verified as a frontend boundary,
rather than requiring F1's smaller timing VM to register it. F3/F4 execute their
registered game-over tails, including F4's plural `AFTER_XXBALLSTS`.

Registered extras are allowed and classified in `analysis/matrix-reachability.json`:

- F2: frontend entries, `LAST_POS`, and unused `TOMTS`.
- F3: frontend entries, `LAST_POS`, data-only `SPINSCOREPTR`, and unused `TOMTS`.
- F4: frontend entries, `LAST_POS`, unused `TOMTS`, legacy `BONUSHELDTS`,

- F1: all 63 gameplay entries are counted; its additional frontend programs live
  in the separate presentation map.

Non-program declaration tables, animation data labels, sentinels, COMMENT blocks
and demo-only source are distinguished from gameplay entries. A declared extra
is not a missing-target failure.

## Stones findings and newly discovered latent crash

STONES uses five Well multiplier records selected by BPOINTER 39..43, not
Gameshow's six-member family. Its GHOSTAREA contains eight lamp/routine/collection/
enablement records. The checker extracts those records and the three source
`EFFECT TOWERHUNTn` stages instead of inferring them from Go's ghost array.

All 71 source gameplay destinations were already registered; Stones had no
missing matrix label. Deterministic tests cover all five multiplier transitions,
all eight ghost enable/collect states, the deferred Ghost Hunt/Grim Reaper paths,
Tower Hunt stages, Multi Demons, jackpot/super jackpot, extra ball, Hold Bonus,
Double Bonus, Million/Five/Ten/Fifteen/Twenty Million awards (Fifteen is
MUMMYHEAD), Vault/Well/Tower captures, special-mode continuations, screams,
beaten-high-score bonus, both match outcomes, extra-ball branches, bonus for
multipliers 1/2/4/6/8/10, and the game-over tail.

The packed Tower tests exposed a separate real presentation invariant failure:
`_TOWER` operands `87-16+2`, `61-16+2`, `36-16` were absent from the numeric lookup
map. `stones.numeric()` consequently panicked with `STONES integer 87-16+2`
when the Tower program reached its row comparison. The extractor now retains
the original operand identities and evaluates their source values into
`positions`: 73, 47, 20. Each packed entry is tested through the real matrix tick
until it reaches its exact source row. No runtime expression evaluator, fallback,
award/rule change or row approximation was added.
